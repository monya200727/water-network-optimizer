Water Distribution Network Optimizer

Draw reservoirs, junctions and neighborhoods, connect them with pipes, and compute the maximum water delivery with Edmonds-Karp or Ford-Fulkerson. The tool finds the minimum cut, flags bottleneck pipes and suggests capacity upgrades.

    Failure and vulnerability analysis

    What if a pipe bursts, or a reservoir or junction goes offline? Each element is removed in turn, the maximum flow is recomputed and the damage is ranked. Press Simulate to see the burst on the map.

    Algorithm benchmark

    Random networks of growing size are solved by Ford-Fulkerson, Edmonds-Karp and Dinic. All three must give the same maximum flow; the table and chart show how much work and time each one needs. Complexities: Ford-Fulkerson O(E·f), Edmonds-Karp O(V·E²), Dinic O(V²·E).
